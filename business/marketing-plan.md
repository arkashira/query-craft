## marketing-plan.md – Query‑Craft (90‑Day Go‑to‑Market)

---

### 1️⃣ Positioning  
| Option | One‑liner |
|--------|-----------|
| **A (Primary)** | *“Query‑Craft turns ad‑hoc SQL into a single click – data teams get answers 3× faster and spend more time on insight, not syntax.”* |
| **B** | *“From raw tables to ready‑to‑run queries in seconds – the AI‑assistant that writes, validates and optimizes SQL for analysts.”* |
| **C** | *“Stop rewriting the same joins. Query‑Craft auto‑generates clean, production‑grade SQL from natural‑language questions.”* |
| **D** | *“Your data team’s co‑pilot: type a question, get a vetted query, push to BI – all without a single line of manual code.”* |

| Persona | Name (fictional) | Daily Job & Pain Points | Annual Budget (USD) |

### 5️⃣ Launch Milestones  

| Milestone | Date (relative) | Deliverable |
|----------|----------------|-------------|
| **D‑30** | 30 days before GA | • Closed beta with 5 power‑users (Maya, Liam, Sofia + 2 external) <br>• Finalized pricing (Tiered: Free → $49/mo per user) <br>• Landing page + SEO keywords live |
| **D‑0** (GA) | Launch day | • Public sign‑up opens (14‑day free tier) <br>• Press release to TechCrunch, VentureBeat <br>• Paid‑search & LinkedIn campaigns go live <br>• Community AMA scheduled |
| **D+30** | 1 month post‑launch | • Reach 2 k trial sign‑ups <br>• Convert ≥ 10 % to paid (target $9.8 k MRR) <br>• Publish first case study <br>• Iterate onboarding flow based on NPS > 40 |
| **D+90** | 3 months post‑launch | • 5 k active users (DAU ≥ 1 k) <br>• $45 k MRR (≈ 4.5 % conversion) <br>• CAC ≤ $115, LTV ≥ $1.2 k (12 mo) <br>• Secure 2 enterprise pilot contracts (>$5 k ARR each) |

---

### 6️⃣ Success Metrics (KPIs)

| Metric | Target (by D+90) |
|--------|------------------|
| **Daily Active Users (DAU)** | **1,200** (≥ 24 % of trial users) |
| **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)** | **$45,000** |
| **Conversion Rate (Free → Paid)** | **≥ 10 %** |
| **CAC Payback Period** | **≤ 3 months** |
| **Net Promoter Score (NPS)** | **≥ 45** |
| **Referral‑Generated Sign‑ups** | **≥ 15 %** of new trials |

*If any metric falls > 15 % short of target at D+30, trigger a rapid‑iteration sprint (content refresh, pricing test, or channel re‑allocation).*
